## What would broaden this library next?

Choose a gap before collecting more links: a dense documentation site, a complex accessible form, a content-heavy publication, a useful data dashboard, or a low-bandwidth service. A spectacular immersive site is not automatically the right model for these jobs. New URLs remain candidates until inspected and documented.

For each candidate, capture one transferable design relationship, its evidence, and an implementation constraint:

```text
Primary URL and final URL if redirected:
Inspected date and viewport:
Evidence method: text / screenshot / interaction / measured performance
Observed composition:
Observed typography and density:
Observed interaction and alternate state:
Hypothesis worth trying in an original design:
Risk or constraint to investigate:
What was not verified:
```

Follow the [reference workflow](../docs/REFERENCE-WORKFLOW.md). Keep links and original analysis; do not import another studio's source, screenshots, fonts, video, or artwork into a training dataset without appropriate rights.
