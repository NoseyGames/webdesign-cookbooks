# OnEarth — let landscape establish the subject

Source: [OnEarth template](https://onearth-conservation.webflow.io/) · Reviewed: 2026-10-21 (America/Los_Angeles)

## Evidence and scope

**Visual-reviewed.** Inspected the desktop hero and its rendered page structure. The page title identifies this as a **Webflow ecommerce website template**. Project dates, impact totals, people, and donations are template content and must not be treated as verified nonprofit results. The text-fetch tool failed, but direct browser inspection succeeded.

## Observed

A full-bleed aerial forest-and-road image fills the opening view. A translucent rounded navigation bar floats near the top, with an opaque pill marking the selected page. Large white conservation messaging appears over the landscape. The page structure proceeds through mission, initiatives, impact figures, projects, partners, campaigns, team, and FAQ. Some initiative descriptions repeat, demonstrating why demo content needs editorial review before reuse.

## Transferable lesson

**Interpretation:** a strong environment photograph can communicate subject and emotional context before the visitor reads. The rest of the page attempts to convert that feeling into concrete avenues for involvement. The useful sequence is mission → work → evidence → participation; the sample statistics are not reusable evidence.

## Implementation suggestion

Use licensed or original photography with deliberate cropping, a stable background color, and a contrast overlay. Build campaign summaries from real structured data with place, activity, status, and a clear action. Keep the navigation readable without requiring blur support. These are proposed implementation choices.

## Accessible adaptation and limits

Provide a still image for any optional motion, respect reduced-motion preferences, and preserve readable text over every crop. Use a logical heading outline rather than copying the template's multiple large heading levels. A cinematic hero is a poor fit when visitors urgently need eligibility, emergency guidance, or a short factual answer.
