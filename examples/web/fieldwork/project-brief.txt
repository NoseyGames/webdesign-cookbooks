YOUR NEXT PROJECT
=================

1. What are you making, and who is it for?


2. What should someone understand or do after visiting the website?


3. What problem are you trying to solve?


4. Which content and assets do you already have permission to use?


5. Share three references. Explain one specific thing you appreciate in each.


6. What must the website do? Include forms, integrations, and content editing.


7. What constraints matter? Think about timeline, budget, accessibility,
   languages, existing technology, performance, and maintenance.


8. How will you know the project succeeded?


This template is part of webdesign-cookbooks. Fieldwork is a fictional studio.
Nothing is submitted or collected by the example website.
