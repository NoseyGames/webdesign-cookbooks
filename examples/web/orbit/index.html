02 / Field notes

Stay for
the details.

Three fictional installations. Three prompts for looking at an ordinary day differently.

01

Light / Perception

The Observatory

A slowly changing circle of light asks you to notice the edges, then the space between them. Imagine the same shape at dawn and at midnight.

Open the observation prompt

Choose an everyday object. Draw its shadow three times during a day. Which properties stay constant, and which belong to the light?

02

Memory / Collection

The Archive

An imagined collection of small, overlooked things. Its value comes from the connections you make, rather than the rarity of any one object.

Open the collection prompt

Gather five ordinary objects with a shared texture. Arrange them by a different property. Describe what the second arrangement reveals.

03

Time / Emergence

The Garden

A study in repetition with variation. A familiar form changes slightly each time it appears, suggesting a living system without recreating one.

Open the pattern prompt

Repeat a simple mark twenty times, changing only its angle. Find the point where repetition starts to look like movement.

Make something from what you notice.
