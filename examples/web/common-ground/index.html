After Hours

01

A neighborhood cinema with a life beyond the last frame.

Identity / Digital · Cultural spaces

Read case notes: After Hours

Challenge. Give a fictional independent cinema a clear way to promote films, talks, and late-night gatherings.

Decision. A cropped circular symbol suggests both a projector and a meeting place. Event dates remain live text, with a consistent booking route.

Concept outcome. An adaptable identity and proposed event index. This is invented work; attendance and booking results have not been measured.

Good Loop

02

Repair, reuse, repeat. A fresh identity for a local repair collective.

Identity / Motion · Circular living

Read case notes: Good Loop

Challenge. Make a fictional repair service feel welcoming to people who have never fixed anything.

Decision. Interlocking loops express exchange. A three-step repair story would animate only on request and always have a static equivalent.

Concept outcome. A visual identity and motion direction, represented here by an original still. No video, real service, or customer outcome is implied.

Open Index

03

An editorial home for ideas that deserve a second life.

Digital · Independent publishing

Read case notes: Open Index

Challenge. Help readers find useful essays in an invented independent publication.

Decision. Use a clear typographic index, descriptive article titles, and persistent topic labels. Reading comes before decoration.

Concept outcome. A proposed editorial system. The miniature cover above is an illustration, not a working article browser; this page's project filters are the live example.

Common Frequency

04

A visual rhythm for a community radio festival.

Motion · Arts & sound

Read case notes: Common Frequency

Challenge. Connect a fictional festival's different voices without giving each one a separate visual system.

Decision. Repeated wave geometry creates a shared rhythm. Any future animation would accompany the program rather than delay access to it.

Concept outcome. An original poster and motion storyboard direction. This demo intentionally uses a still and makes no claim about a produced film or audience response.

Table Talk

05

An invitation to meet your neighbors over something good.

Identity · Food & community

Read case notes: Table Talk

Challenge. Make an invented neighborhood supper club feel open to first-time guests.

Decision. Chairs and a shared table become simple geometric building blocks. Practical event information sits beside artwork, never embedded within it.

Concept outcome. A flexible print and social identity. The project is fictional and has no real event reservations or measured participation.

Small Hours

06

A calmer digital place for stories at the end of the day.

Digital / Motion · Reading & wellbeing

Read case notes: Small Hours

Challenge. Introduce a fictional evening reading collection without adding more noise to the day.

Decision. Use quiet spacing, a readable text mode, and optional short transitions. Do not auto-play audio or make motion necessary for navigation.

Concept outcome. A digital art direction illustrated here with a static night motif. There are no medical claims, real subscribers, or completed app behind this concept.

Common ground.

Good work starts with a shared question. We bring identity, digital design, and motion into the same conversation, then make the answer clear enough to travel.

Common Ground is a fictional practice. All six projects, illustrations, and case notes were created for this cookbook.

How to start a project ↗

Start with a good question.

What could we
make together?

For your own practice, replace this with a real contact route. Begin a useful brief with:

  1. Who you're trying to reach.
  2. What they need to do or understand.
  3. Your scope, constraints, and timeline.
